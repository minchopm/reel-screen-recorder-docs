# Reel: Screen Recorder Face Cam — frequently asked questions

## Can I record app sound and my voice together?

Yes, that is a documented workflow. Enable the relevant inputs and verify the saved result with a short test. Some apps or protected media may restrict capture.

## Do I need cloud hosting to share a recording?

Reel saves a local MP4. You can choose your own sharing destination after export; that destination has its own data handling and size limits.

## Can I change microphone volume separately?

Yes. The default exported mix is one stereo track, so choose a useful balance before recording.

## Does the free version include facecam?

The current public site includes facecam, audio controls and background effects in the free ad-supported tier. Pro adds native resolution and removes ads.

## Why is the exported video smaller than my screen?

The selected quality tier can scale the capture while preserving its aspect ratio. Source (Native) is a Pro option.

## Is a recorded marketing video automatically an App Preview?

No. Capture is only the source material. Review the current requirements of the intended Apple placement before submitting; a decorative framed marketing composition is not automatically an eligible App Preview.

## Where should I start?

Use the quick-start guide with a small example, verify the saved result, then choose a practical workflow. Avoid beginning with irreplaceable data or an entire production project.

## How do I report a documentation or app problem?

Use the official product support channel for account or purchase issues. A public GitHub issue is suitable for documentation corrections and sanitized reproduction steps. Include the app version, device/OS, expected outcome and actual result; do not attach private data.
