# Recording acceptance checklist

This is an operational check, not a performance benchmark or an approval certificate.

## Before capture

Confirm destination aspect ratio, quality tier, camera placement, permissions and the intended microphone/output route. Prepare sample data and suppress unrelated notifications.

## Test sequence

Record speech alone, app sound alone and speech over app sound. Visit every screen in the planned route. Stop and play the actual saved MP4.

## Pass criteria

Speech is intelligible; app sound is present when required; there is no doubled audio; the overlay does not hide actions; orientation stays correct; no private content appears.

## Before delivery

Open the file in the receiving player, inspect start/middle/end and check upload limits. Keep the original export until delivery is verified.

## Evidence to retain

App version, OS/device, selected quality/orientation, audio route, approximate duration, source app and the observed failure timestamp. Do not claim a fixed file-size or performance result without measuring it.
