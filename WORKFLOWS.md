# Reel: Screen Recorder Face Cam — practical workflows

These are suggested user procedures, not claims that automated tests have been performed. Each workflow has a concrete outcome to inspect.

## A narrated app tutorial

Prepare three actions: open a sample item, change one setting, and show the result. Keep the camera in a corner that remains clear for all three. Say what the viewer should notice before each action, pause briefly, then perform it. Review the recording for hidden controls and accidental notifications. The deliverable is an MP4 that explains one repeatable task.

## A bug report with useful evidence

Use dummy content and record the shortest path from a known starting state to the problem. Say the expected behavior, perform the trigger once, and leave the result visible. Include app and OS versions in the report text rather than exposing account details on screen. Check that the recording includes the failing action; do not repeat the error just to make a longer video.

## A Mac presentation with facecam

Prepare the display or window content before starting. Keep recorder controls and unrelated windows out of the demonstration. Record a short test while the source plays audio, then speak over it and compare both levels. Watch the exported result to verify the actual composition; a preview alone does not prove what was captured.

## A vertical reaction or commentary clip

Choose portrait framing before the first take. Use media you have permission to record and comment on. Headphones help avoid speaker audio being captured again by the microphone. Pause the source when you need to explain a point. Review voice intelligibility and camera placement at the busiest moment of the video.
